## Findings

- Two clones of Claude reported 1.48 GB allocated in total against a 741.1 MB source.
- `du` counts a copy-on-write clone's shared blocks against the clone, so the number above is an upper bound, not additional space consumed. The volume's free space is the honest measure, and APFS clones cost near zero until the file diverges.
- A profile directory after one short launch was 10.3 MB. This is not the steady-state figure — an Electron profile grows to hundreds of megabytes with normal use, and Claude Desktop additionally bootstraps a local runtime image, which is why the UI quotes 2 GB for it.
- The product measures this per-instance at runtime rather than estimating, and the compatibility card quotes 367 MB for a typical Electron app and 2.15 GB for a heavy one.
